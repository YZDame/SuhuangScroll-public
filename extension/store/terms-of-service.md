# Suhuang Scroll Bridge Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-08-13

Scroll Runtime. It does not guarantee grading accuracy, website availability,
or compatibility with third-party grading systems. The operator is responsible
for reviewing model decisions, confirming score writeback, and complying with
institutional and legal requirements for student data.

Use of a third-party vision provider is governed by that provider's terms. Do
not use the extension with data you are not authorized to process.
