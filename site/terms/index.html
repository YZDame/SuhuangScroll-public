Suhuang Scroll is an operator-controlled grading Runtime, Codex Plugin, and browser bridge. It does not guarantee grading accuracy, website availability, model availability, or compatibility with third-party grading systems.

Operator responsibility

The operator must be authorized to process the submitted educational data, configure a permitted model provider, review grading decisions, and confirm any score writeback or submission. The system is not a substitute for the institution's academic, legal, or privacy review.

Third-party services

Third-party vision providers and grading websites are governed by their own terms. Adapter behavior can change when those websites change. Keep access credentials private and do not expose them through public issues or logs.

Changes and support

Release-specific limitations and support instructions are maintained on the support page. Use of a hosted relay also requires the deployment operator to provide suitable authentication and retention controls.
