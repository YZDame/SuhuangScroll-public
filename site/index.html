Suhuang Scroll

Grading, with your judgment in control.

A local grading assistant that connects your AI service to supported grading websites. Prepare a marking guide with your AI client, check a few answers, then begin a run when you are ready.

Start with three things

  1. Install Suhuang Scroll on your computer.
  2. Install the Chrome Bridge and sign in to your grading website.
  3. Ask your AI client to turn your question, reference answer and marking guide into a Spec Markdown file.

Read the getting-started guide · Download releases

Keep the workflow familiar

Use the TUI or the optional DeepSeek Harness plugin to choose your Spec, start, pause and review a run. The extension connects the grading page. You do not need to write the Spec yourself.

Check sample results before continuous grading. If you use a remote AI service, answer images are sent to that provider according to your configuration.
